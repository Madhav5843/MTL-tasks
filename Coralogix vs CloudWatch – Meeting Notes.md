# 📒 Coralogix vs CloudWatch – Meeting Notes

## Overview

The team discussed the ongoing adoption of **Coralogix** for monitoring and log analysis. The objective is to gradually transition from relying primarily on **AWS CloudWatch** to using **Coralogix** as the main observability platform for troubleshooting and root cause analysis.

***

## Key Discussion Points

### Coralogix Adoption Strategy

* Coralogix is being introduced as the primary monitoring and log analysis tool.
* AWS CloudWatch will continue to be available and used.
* Over time, Coralogix is expected to provide more detailed insights than CloudWatch.
* The long-term goal is to use Coralogix to identify root causes of issues more quickly and efficiently.

**Key Takeaway:**

> CloudWatch remains available, but Coralogix will become the preferred platform for advanced troubleshooting and root cause analysis.

***

### Existing Alerting Setup

The team reviewed the current alerting mechanism and notification channels.

#### Slack Integration

* An outbound webhook has been configured to send alerts to Slack.
* Slack notifications currently provide detailed operational information.
* Alerts include:
  * Priority level
  * Threshold conditions
  * Impacted nodes
  * Additional diagnostics

#### Observation

Slack alerts currently contain significantly more information than OpsGenie alerts.

***

### OpsGenie Alert Limitation

During previous investigations, OpsGenie tickets generated by Coralogix were reviewed.

#### Finding

* OpsGenie incidents were being created successfully.
* However, the alerts lacked sufficient diagnostic information.
* The required operational context was not present in the generated tickets.

Examples of missing details:

* Node information
* Threshold conditions
* Extended diagnostic data
* Troubleshooting context

***

### Required Improvement

The team identified a configuration gap within OpsGenie.

#### Action Required

* Review and update the OpsGenie integration for Coralogix alerts.
* Configure alert payload mappings correctly.
* Ensure all relevant alert attributes are passed from Coralogix to OpsGenie.
* Align OpsGenie alerts with the detailed information currently available in Slack notifications.
