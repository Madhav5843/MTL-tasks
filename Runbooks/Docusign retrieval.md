## DocuSign Document Retrieval Process

### Overview

DocuSign documents are customer-signed electronic documents that are stored within the DocuSign platform. Access to these documents is restricted for security and compliance purposes, and retrieval is managed through the OMS (Operations Management Services) team.

***

## End-to-End Workflow

### 1. Document Request Initiated

A customer or internal user (e.g., **Harry**) requires a signed DocuSign document related to a specific application.

* Customers sign required documents electronically through **DocuSign** when applying for Monument services.
* The signed documents are stored within DocuSign and are not directly accessible to all users.

### 2. PSD Ticket Raised

Since Harry does not have direct access to retrieve documents from DocuSign:

* Harry raises a **PSD Service Request/Ticket**.
* The request includes the relevant application number (e.g., **APP924**).

### 3. Request Forwarded to OMS Team

The support team also does not have direct DocuSign access.

* The request is forwarded to the **OMS team** (e.g., Neal Jannels).
* The application number and document requirements are provided for retrieval.

### 4. OMS Team Retrieves the Document

The OMS team performs the following actions:

1. Logs into **DocuSign** (or the associated document management system).
2. Searches for the signed document linked to the application number.
3. Downloads the required signed PDF document.
4. Uploads the document to the appropriate customer/document repository where the requester can access it.

### 5. Request Completion

Once OMS confirms the upload:

* The support team notifies Harry that the document is available.
* Harry is asked to verify that the uploaded document is the correct file.

***

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Requester (Harry)

* Identifies the need for a DocuSign document.
* Raises a PSD ticket requesting document retrieval.
* Verifies the document once it has been uploaded.

### Support Team

* Acts as the coordinator between the requester and OMS.
* Forwards retrieval requests to OMS.
* Tracks progress and communicates updates to the requester.

### OMS Team

* Has authorized access to DocuSign.
* Retrieves signed documents from the system.
* Uploads documents to the designated repository for user access.

***

## Purpose of This Process

Access to customer-signed legal documents is restricted due to **security, privacy, and compliance requirements**.

As a result:

* Requesters do not have direct access to DocuSign.
* Support teams facilitate the request process.
* Only authorized OMS personnel can retrieve and distribute signed documents.

This controlled workflow ensures that sensitive customer documentation is accessed and handled only by authorized individuals.
